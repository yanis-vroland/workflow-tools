# Brief: field service scheduling tool

Demo Heating Ltd (fictitious company), boiler maintenance
Date: 1 October 2026

## Background

Our 25 technicians are scheduled in a shared spreadsheet by a single dispatcher.

## Requirements

1. Dispatchers schedule jobs and assign them to a technician by area (must have).
2. Technicians record a job report that is emailed to the customer.
3. Monthly dashboard for management (nice to have).

## Constraints

- The app must be fast.
- Customer data must stay in the EU.

## Out of scope

Invoicing is not in scope.
